COLD OPEN

ARRIVAL — before Phase 1

Cutscene, plays before players spawn. A small Orion delegation (not yet billed as "Hades Platoon") lands at Sotilaslentokenttä — Finnish for "military airfield," cover name for real-world Wehrheim, same convention as Ahola/Korpiharju/Peltola — meets Major General Virtanen, and rides an unmarked convoy to the FOB on Altkönig (a real, out-of-the-way hill near Kronberg/Königstein) while he delivers the threat briefing en route. Cutscene ends at the FOB; Phase 1 starts there instead of after a 20-minute drive.

Full shot-by-shot script with all dialogue lives outside this dossier: Arma3 Orion - Finland Campagne - Script Op1 Cold Open.md — that file is the working source, this page just tracks the beat.

OP 1

SIGNAL FIRE — Ahola

Destroy Russian forward observer posts around Ahola, then secure the FDF evacuation out of the village.

PHASE 1 / 4

Recon & take-down

Take out two observer teams: a high point with a laser designator, a radio post by the forest track. A quiet direct-action opening phase — first contact with a war that isn't even 72 hours old.

PHASE 2 / 4

Intercept the fire plan

Small command post by the forest track. Intercept the fire plan, destroy the mortar ammunition earmarked for Ahola before it hits the village.

PHASE 3 / 4

Escort

Escort the FDF column — reservists, not regulars — from a rally point through the village to the withdrawal road. Väinö Ahola and the Korhonen family are among the civilians evacuating.

PHASE 4 / 4

Fighting withdrawal

Hold the road open while Russian scouts and a light QRF push in. The column isn't clear yet — this isn't a mop-up anymore, this is holding the line.

COMBAT CLIMAX

Column stuck

The column is stuck at a disabled vehicle. Choice under fire: secure the road for civilians and FDF, or take out the mortar team hitting the exfil route first. Both options cost time nobody has.

NEWS

How the world sees it (in-fiction) — the week of Signal Fire

T-6 DAYS — SIX DAYS BEFORE THE CROSSING
MINISTRY OF DEFENCE, RUSSIAN FEDERATION — PRESS SERVICE / ПРЕСС-СЛУЖБА

«Плановые манёвры 44-й армии у финской границы — рутинная зимняя подготовка»

The Russian Ministry of Defence announced Monday that the 44th Army Corps will conduct "scheduled winter readiness exercises" in the Leningrad and Karelia military districts, describing the manoeuvres as routine and pre-announced under existing transparency agreements. The ministry noted this is the corps' second such exercise this year. NATO monitoring stations report troop and equipment movement consistent with the announcement, though analysts note the scale is larger than previous winter drills.

AP — EASTERN EUROPE BUREAU

"Russia says border drills 'routine,' but scale raises quiet questions in Helsinki"

Russia's announcement of winter exercises near the Finnish border drew a muted official response from Helsinki, where defence officials said only that movements were "being monitored in line with standard procedure." Off the record, one FDF officer described the exercise footprint as "larger than the calendar we were given," though no formal statement has been made. The exercises are set to run for two weeks. Opposition MPs have called for a briefing; the government has not yet scheduled one.

T-3 DAYS — BEFORE THE CROSSING
YLE NEWS — NATIONAL DESK

"Finland raises readiness along eastern border amid Russian troop movements"

The Finnish Defence Forces have quietly raised readiness levels in South Karelia, officials confirmed Tuesday, citing "unusual concentrations" tied to Russia's 44th Army Corps near Petrozavodsk. The Ministry of Defence declined to call the movements a threat, pointing to the corps' ongoing, unfinished build-up. Analysts note the timing follows closely on NATO's Cold Response exercises. "We watch. We do not panic," one MoD spokesperson said. "We have done this before."

T-0 — THE MORNING OF THE CROSSING
KARJALAN SANOMAT — SOUTH KARELIA EDITION

"Unrest at border village Ahola, authorities urge calm"

Residents of the border village of Ahola report heavy blasts in the early morning. The Finnish Defence Forces confirm "heightened readiness" in the region but stop short of calling it a formal attack. Local elder Väinö Ahola: "We've been through this before. We know what it means when the roads close."

TASS — ГРАНИЧНАЯ ЗОНА / BORDER ZONE

"Западные наёмники обстреляли мирное население у финской границы"

Sources within the 44th Army Corps report that unidentified armed units — presumed Western private contractors — are responsible for explosions near Ahola alleged to have caused civilian casualties. Moscow calls it "a direct provocation that justifies retaliation."

REUTERS — WIRE / 06:41 GMT

"Finland invokes Article 5 as Russian forces cross border near Ahola"

Finland formally invoked NATO's Article 5 collective-defence clause on Thursday after confirming Russian units crossed into South Karelia, alliance officials said, though a joint response has yet to be agreed. Unconfirmed local reports describe armed personnel operating alongside Finnish reservists near Ahola who "did not match any known FDF unit" — a detail NATO officials declined to comment on. "This is moving faster than our process," one diplomat said on condition of anonymity.

T+2 DAYS — AFTER
TELEGRAM — "ЗАПАД-ФРОНТ" (UNOFFICIAL MILBLOGGER CHANNEL) · 4.2K SUBSCRIBERS

"Тени у Ахолы" — "Shadows at Ahola"

> lads, don't believe the Ministry's press release, our boys at Ahola did NOT have an "easy morning of consolidation." Grom-6 lost a vehicle at the crossing and the observer teams went dark before anyone official wants to admit. Whoever hit them wasn't FDF conscripts, the shooting was too clean.
